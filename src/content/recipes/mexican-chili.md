## Steps

1. Dice all vegetables and set aside.
2. In a large pot, add vegetables and cook until softened.
3. Add ground beef, break it up, and brown. Stir in mild spices and tomato paste.
4. Add wheat beer, Worcestershire sauce, tomato puree, beef stock, and brown sugar. Optionally add Mexican chocolate for a unique flavor. Simmer on low heat for 3-4 hours, stirring occasionally.
5. Serve warm topped with avocado slices, a dollop of sour cream, and fresh cilantro.

---

![Mexican chili in a pot on the stove](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1583636914650-e21f8f4e5c60?w=600&q=80)

This chili benefits from a long, gentle simmer—the flavors deepen and the beef becomes fork-tender. The wheat beer adds malty depth; Mexican chocolate, if you add it, brings a subtle warmth and hint of spice.

For a gluten-free version, omit the Worcestershire sauce or use a gluten-free brand, and swap the wheat beer for a gluten-free beer or extra beef stock. The chili keeps and freezes well, and often tastes even better the next day.

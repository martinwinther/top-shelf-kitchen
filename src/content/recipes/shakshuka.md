## Steps

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium. Add onion and bell peppers and cook until softened, about 5–7 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and cayenne. Stir 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add canned tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer 10–15 minutes until the sauce has thickened. Adjust seasoning.
4. Make 4 wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover the pan and cook 5–7 minutes until the egg whites are set and yolks are still runny (or to your liking).
5. Sprinkle with cilantro or parsley and serve straight from the pan with bread for dipping.

![Shakshuka with eggs and herbs](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1575932444877-5106bee2a599?w=600&q=80)

Shakshuka is endlessly adaptable: add feta, spinach, or harissa for extra flavor. The sauce can be made ahead and reheated before adding the eggs.

Use a skillet that can go from stove to table for a relaxed brunch. If you prefer set yolks, leave the lid on a bit longer. Leftover sauce keeps in the fridge for a few days—reheat and add fresh eggs when you’re ready.

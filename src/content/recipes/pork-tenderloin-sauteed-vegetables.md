## Steps

1. Trim the pork tenderloin and season thoroughly with salt. Heat a pan over medium-high, add half of the olive oil, and sear the pork 90 seconds on each side until browned. Transfer to a baking sheet.
2. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 13–15 minutes.
3. While the pork bakes, peel and cube the potatoes into bite-sized pieces. Microwave the potatoes 5 minutes at 600W to par-cook.
4. Chop the onion and zucchini.
5. In a glass, combine soy sauce, honey, balsamic vinegar (or rice vinegar), and cornstarch. Stir well until cornstarch is dissolved.
6. In a large pan over medium heat, add the remaining olive oil and the microwaved potatoes with a pinch of salt. Fry 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly browned.
7. Add a bit more oil if needed, then add onion and zucchini. Season with another pinch of salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Cook, stirring, 3–4 minutes until vegetables are tender.
8. Remove the pork from the oven and let rest 3–4 minutes before slicing. Serve pork alongside the sautéed vegetables and drizzle with the prepared sauce.

![Pork tenderloin and vegetables on a plate](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603360946369-dc9bb6258143?w=600&q=80)

Searing the pork before baking keeps it juicy and gives a good crust. Resting it briefly before slicing helps the juices redistribute.

The sauce can be warmed in a small pan if you prefer it hot, or use it as-is. Par-cooking the potatoes in the microwave speeds up browning in the pan. Swap zucchini for other vegetables (e.g. bell pepper, green beans) if you like.

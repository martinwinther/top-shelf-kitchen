## Steps

1. Rinse the rice and mung dal until the water runs clear. Drain.
2. Melt ghee in a medium pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add turmeric, coriander, ginger, and cardamom (if using). If using onion, add it and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the rice and dal and stir to coat in the ghee and spices. Pour in the water and add salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer and cover.
4. Cook 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally and adding a little more water if it gets too thick, until the dal and rice are soft and the mixture is porridge-like.
5. Taste and add more salt if needed. Serve warm, topped with cilantro and a little extra ghee if you like. Add lime wedges on the side for those who want a touch of acid.

In Ayurveda, kitchari is considered tridoshic and especially grounding for **Vata**: it’s warm, moist, cooked, and easy to digest. The combination of rice and mung dal with ghee and warming spices (cumin, turmeric, ginger, cardamom) supports stability and nourishment without being heavy or raw.

Eat it when you need something simple and soothing, or as a gentle reset. You can add well-cooked vegetables like zucchini or carrot in the last 15 minutes if you like. Avoid overdoing cold drinks, raw salads, or dry snacks alongside it if you’re focusing on balancing Vata.

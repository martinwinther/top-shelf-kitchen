## Steps

1. In a wide, shallow bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, vanilla, cinnamon, sugar, and salt.
2. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add half the butter.
3. Dip each bread slice into the egg mixture, letting it soak 10–15 seconds per side so it absorbs the custard but doesn’t fall apart.
4. Place the soaked slices in the pan and cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden and set. Add the remaining butter before the second batch if needed.
5. Serve hot with maple syrup, fresh berries, or a dusting of icing sugar.

![French toast in a pan](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1499636136210-6f4ee915583e?w=600&q=80)

Slightly stale or day-old bread holds up best and soaks up the custard without going mushy. Thick slices (e.g. brioche or challah) give a rich, soft result; standard sandwich bread works too.

Keep the heat at medium so the egg sets without burning. You can keep finished slices warm in a low oven while you cook the rest. For a lighter version, use a little less egg and more milk.

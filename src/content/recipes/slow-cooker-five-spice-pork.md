## Steps

1. **Prep the meat:** Remove rind (keep a thin fat cap). Pat the pork dry. Rub all over with the dry rub (salt, brown sugar, five-spice, white pepper, and optional garlic powder). Rest 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
2. **Load the slow cooker:** Put spring onions and star anise (and orange peel if using) in the bottom. Lay the whole pork on top. Mix the braising liquid (soy, hoisin, vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic, wine if using, water or stock, chili) and pour around—not over—the meat. Keep the crock no more than two-thirds full.
3. **Cook:** LOW 10–12 hours (typically ~11), or HIGH 6½–8 hours. Done when a fork slides in with little resistance and internal temp is 93–96°C. If one side isn’t in the liquid, spoon liquid over the top a few times or flip once halfway if your cooker allows.
4. **Rest and shred:** Lift the pork out, rest 15–20 minutes, then shred. Skim fat from the pot juices.
5. **Reduce and glaze:** Boil the juices hard until reduced by about half. Whisk in 1–2 tbsp hoisin. For a thicker glaze, drizzle in a slurry of 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water, simmer gently 30–90 seconds until glossy. Balance with salt, sweet, or vinegar as needed.
6. **Optional crisp edges:** Toss shredded pork with some sauce, spread on a tray, and grill/broil at 240°C for 5–8 minutes until caramelised. Toss with a little more sauce to glaze. Great for bao or rice bowls.

![Shredded pork with glaze](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603360946369-dc9bb6258143?w=600&q=80)

Use a single piece of pork (about 2 kg) so it braises evenly. The pork will release juice—no need to top up the liquid. If the piece is very tall and touches the lid, tent with foil under the lid or trim a sliver; otherwise keep it whole.

Expected yield is about 1.3–1.5 kg shredded pork. It freezes well. The optional broiled step gives caramelised edges that work especially well in bao buns or over rice.

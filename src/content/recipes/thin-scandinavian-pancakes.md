## Steps

1. In a bowl, using an electric mixer, whisk together flour, sugar, vanilla sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, and salt.
2. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing until smooth.
3. Gradually whisk in the milk until the batter is lump-free.
4. Let the batter rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.
5. Heat a medium pan with a little butter to medium heat. Pour in a thin layer of batter, swirling to coat.
6. Cook until golden on both sides, adding more butter between each pancake.

![Thin Scandinavian pancakes being cooked in a pan](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551782450-17144efb9c50?w=600&q=80)

These delicate pancakes can be enjoyed as both breakfast and dessert, depending on your toppings. Serve them with jam, fresh berries, whipped cream, ice cream, or simply with a sprinkle of sugar. The cardamom adds a warm, aromatic flavor that's characteristic of Scandinavian baking.

The batter benefits from resting—it allows the flour to fully hydrate and results in a smoother texture. If you don't have vanilla sugar, vanilla extract works just as well. Adjust the cardamom to your taste, or omit it if you prefer a more neutral flavor.

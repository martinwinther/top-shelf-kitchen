## Steps

1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, and baking soda.
2. In another bowl, beat together buttermilk, oil, vanilla extract, and eggs until well combined.
3. Gradually mix the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients until just combined (avoid over-mixing).
4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
5. Cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry, then flip and cook until browned on the other side.
6. Serve hot with your choice of toppings such as syrup, butter, berries, or whipped cream.

![Fluffy American pancakes cooking on a griddle](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528207776546-365bb710ee93?w=600&q=80)

These classic American pancakes are light, fluffy, and perfect for weekend breakfasts. The buttermilk adds a subtle tang and helps create that signature tender texture.

The key to fluffy pancakes is not over-mixing the batter—a few lumps are perfectly fine. Let the batter rest for a minute or two after mixing to allow the baking powder to activate. Cook over medium-high heat for the best golden-brown color without burning.

## Steps

1. Make sure your milk and eggs are at room temperature. If the eggs are cold, rest them in warm water for 20 minutes.
2. In a large bowl, mix together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
3. In a separate jug or bowl, whisk together the milk and eggs until combined.
4. Pour the egg-milk mixture into the dry ingredients, then add the melted butter.
5. Whisk until you have a smooth, thick batter. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
6. Preheat your waffle iron according to its manual.
7. Pour in enough batter to mostly fill the waffle mold. Close and cook per the machine's instructions.
8. Serve warm. Makes 6–8 waffles (using a heart-shaped waffle maker).

![Waffles cooking in a waffle iron](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1562376552-0d160a2f238d?w=600&q=80)

These waffles are crispy on the outside and tender on the inside. The key is using room-temperature ingredients and letting the batter rest—this allows the flour to hydrate and creates a better texture.

Don't overfill the waffle iron; the batter will expand as it cooks. The first waffle is often a test—adjust the amount of batter and cooking time based on your machine. Serve immediately while hot and crispy, with your favorite toppings like maple syrup, fresh berries, or whipped cream.

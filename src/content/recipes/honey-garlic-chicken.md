## Steps

1. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken and cook 5–6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. In the same pan (lower heat to medium), add garlic and stir 30 seconds. Add honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and sriracha if using. Bring to a simmer and cook 1–2 minutes until slightly thickened. Optional: stir in cornstarch slurry for a thicker glaze.
4. Return the chicken to the pan and turn to coat in the sauce. Cook 1–2 minutes.
5. Serve with rice or greens, drizzled with the glaze. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion if you like.

![Honey garlic chicken with rice](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603360946369-dc9bb6258143?w=600&q=80)

This glaze keeps well: double the sauce ingredients and store half in the fridge for another night. Skin-on thighs stay juicier; skinless works if you prefer less fat.

If the sauce gets too dark in the pan, add a splash of water. Serve with steamed rice, broccoli, or a simple salad.

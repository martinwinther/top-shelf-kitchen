## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
2. In a pan, heat oil and cook the onion until soft. Add minced beef and mushrooms, cooking until beef is browned.
3. Sprinkle flour over the beef mixture, stir to coat, then pour in broth and simmer until thickened.
4. Spoon the beef mixture into a pie dish.
5. Boil 500 g potatoes until tender, mash with butter, salt, and pepper. Spread the mashed potatoes over the beef mixture.
6. Cover with pie crust, sealing the edges. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the pastry is golden.

This comforting pie combines rich beef and earthy mushrooms with a creamy mashed potato layer and golden pastry crust. Perfect for a cozy dinner on a cold evening.

The key to a great pie is ensuring the filling is well-seasoned and not too wet—the flour helps thicken the broth to the right consistency. You can use either store-bought pie crust or puff pastry, depending on your preference. The mashed potato layer adds extra creaminess and makes this pie particularly satisfying.

This dish can be assembled ahead of time and kept in the fridge until ready to bake, making it great for meal prep or entertaining.

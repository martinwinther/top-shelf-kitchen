## Steps

1. In a large bowl, combine chickpeas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, feta, olives, and red onion.
2. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Pour the dressing over the chickpea mixture and toss gently.
4. Divide between bowls, garnish with parsley or dill, and serve with pita or bread if you like.

![Chickpea salad in a bowl](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512621776951-a57141f2eefd?w=600&q=80)

This bowl keeps well in the fridge for a day—dress it just before eating so the cucumber and tomatoes stay crisp. Add cooked quinoa or couscous for extra bulk, or a soft-boiled egg for more protein.

Use the chickpea liquid (aquafaba) from the can for other recipes, or discard. For a stronger flavor, add a pinch of sumac or a little minced garlic to the dressing.
